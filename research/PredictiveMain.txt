Predictive Maintenance

Definition: AI predicts failures before they happen using sensor data.

Sensors: Temperature, Pressure, Vibration, RPM, Torque, Voltage,
Current, Oil Quality, Operating Hours.

Outputs: Failure Probability Machine Health Score Remaining Useful Life
(RUL)

Example: Temperature: 84 C Vibration: 5.6 mm/s Failure Probability: 91%
Health Score: 42% RUL: 35 hrs

Decision: <30% Healthy 30-60% Monitor 60-80% Maintenance Soon >80%
Immediate Maintenance
