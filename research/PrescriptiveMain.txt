Prescriptive Maintenance

Definition: AI predicts failures and recommends the best maintenance
action.

Example: Temperature: 92 C Vibration: 6.3 mm/s Current: 19 A Failure
Probability: 96% RUL: 18 hrs

Recommendations: - Stop machine - Replace bearing - Lubricate shaft -
Check motor alignment - Inspect cooling system

Decision Rules: Temperature >85 C -> Cooling inspection Vibration >5
mm/s -> Bearing replacement RUL <50 hrs -> Replace component Failure
Probability >90% -> Immediate shutdown
