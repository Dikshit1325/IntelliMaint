Preventive Maintenance

Definition: Maintenance is scheduled at fixed intervals regardless of
machine condition.

Workflow: Operating Hours -> Scheduled Service -> Replace Parts ->
Resume Operation

Example Thresholds: Operating Hours: >500 hrs Oil Quality: <60% Service
Interval: Every 30 days Bearing Inspection: Every 750 hrs

Decision Rule: Maintenance Required if Operating Hours > 500 OR Oil
Quality < 60%.
